Research

My DPhil thesis examines the impact of democratic innovations on the quality of governance in Europe, with a focus on participatory and deliberative tools. I draw on the methods from comparative politics and political economy to analyze the effects of these innovations on citizen engagement, policy responsiveness, and regime support. My work contributes to debates on democratic resilience and the future of democracy in an era of rising authoritarianism.
