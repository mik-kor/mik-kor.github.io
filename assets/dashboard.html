What is a party average? For each day, every account in the party gets its own value for the chosen metric (for example, its average likes per post that day). The party line is the simple mean of those account values. Each account counts equally, so the party account and a high-profile leader weigh the same as a less-followed MP. Accounts that didn’t post on a given day are left out of that day’s average.

Date range of posts per account in this dataset. Accounts with no posts before 8 October can’t show a before/after change.
